About BMK Group

Built on trust. Driven by innovation.

BMK Group has been a trusted leader across the UAE and international consumer electronics landscape since 2002. Originating as Island Valley Electronics LLC, it has evolved into a diversified global enterprise: global sourcing, authorized tier-one brand distribution, regional wholesale trade, retail showrooms and original product manufacturing.

Today the group operates corporate entities, retail outlets and distribution hubs across six markets: the UAE, Singapore, Malaysia, Zambia, the DR Congo and India.

        Our Journey

        From global trade to new possibilities.

        A business built on international connections, evolving with the technology market and expanding into new opportunities.

        1. 2002

          Origins & Early Success

          Founded as Island Valley Electronics LLC in Dubai, trading consumer electronics.

        2. 2004

          Portfolio Diversification

          Wholesale and retail expand into digital imaging and cameras.

        3. 2010

          International Expansion

          BMK GULF FZE in JAFZA and BMK Electronics in Singapore.

        4. 2013

          Launch of SEEKEN

          Flagship brand SEEKEN launches in Singapore with the ST100 tablet.

        6. 2022

          Wearables & Premium Segments

          SEEKEN moves into premium smartwatches and advanced audio.

        7. 2023

          Acquisition of TEAM Appliances

          BMK acquires home appliance brand TEAM Appliances.
